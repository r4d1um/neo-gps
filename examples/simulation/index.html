What the simulation shows

  • GPS errors from the atmosphere and satellite orbits are almost the same for receivers close together. If all four sensors measure at the same time, these errors shift the whole quadrilateral but change neither area nor slope.
  • A single device carried from corner to corner measures each corner at a different time. The common error drifts during that time and distorts the shape. Measuring longer only helps after many hours.
  • What remains is multipath reception (reflections off house, fence, trees) at each individual spot. It largely averages out over hours, a residue remains. That is why the slope never gets really accurate: with GPS the height is about twice as uncertain as the position, and an 8 % slope over 30 m is only 2.4 m of height difference.
